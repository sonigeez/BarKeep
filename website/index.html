Seventeen tiny icons want your attention.

BarKeep has other plans.

Three moves. No tutorial academy.

Set it once.
Forget it exists.

  1. 01

    Put up the divider

    Hold Command and drag BarKeep’s divider left of the icons you want managed.

    ◉◆‹‹
  2. 02

    Click, hover, or shortcut

    Use the chevrons, hover over the menu bar, or press Option–Command–B.

    ⌥⌘B
  3. 03

    Enjoy the blank space

    Auto-collapse after five seconds to one minute. Or never. You’re in charge.

    ‹‹

Privacy, minus the interpretive dance

BarKeep can’t spy on your menu bar.

It moves its own native macOS controls. It never inspects, captures, or identifies the icons around them. Deliciously limited software.
